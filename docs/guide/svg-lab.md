# SVG Lab and the Labs page

**Labs** in the sidebar collects the experiments that go beyond the
component library. Each lab has its own controls on the left and reports its
state in the stage bar.

![Labs](/screenshots/labs.png)

## SVG

Procedural decorative graphics rendered from the style's `svgLanguage` and
tokens: mesh, aurora, grid, blobs, waves, rings, glow, noise, liquid and
chrome presets. Corners, stroke weight, gradients, glow and pattern overlays
all follow the style, so the same preset is soft under Claymorphism and
hard-edged under Neo Brutalism. Intensity, density, curvature and animation
are adjustable; animation respects reduced-motion.

## Data visualisation

Line, area, bar (vertical, horizontal, stacked), donut, pie, radial,
sparkline, scatter and waveform charts, all SVG, all reading the style:
curve tension, corner language, glow, gridlines and colours. Hover for
tooltips; series can be toggled.

## Table

Search, sort, filter, pagination, selection, column hiding, row actions, and
every state (loading, empty, error) — under the active style.

## Notifications

A notification centre with read state, counters, actions, grouping and a
bell popover, plus toasts in every variant.

## Motion

Inspect and tune duration, easing, hover and press scale, and translation
across real interactions, with a timeline that shows each curve.

## Material

Fourteen surface materials — flat to liquid — with highlight, shadow,
reflection, texture, blur and transparency broken out so you can see what
each contributes.

## Icons

Outline, filled, duotone, geometric, rounded, 3D, pixel and skeuomorphic
icon treatments applied to the same glyphs, previewed inside buttons,
navigation, cards and inputs.
